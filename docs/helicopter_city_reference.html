      Construction rules (all three types)
        

      • Parameters: w (X extent), d (Z extent), h. Height is snapped to g + n·f (ground floor + n floors), so storeys are always whole. Parapet (0.8 high, 0.5 thick) sits above h.
        • 

      • Fixed-size details: windows, doors, balcony slabs, awnings, sign bands, vents, HVAC boxes and the helipad never scale. Only the wall box scales; detail counts change.
        • 

      • Repetition: bays n = floor((W − 2.4) / pitch) across a facade of width W, leftover split equally as side margins (W − n·pitch)/2 ≥ 1.2. Rows repeat once per floor at pitch f. Ground-floor glazing uses the same rule inside its free span.
        • 

      • Doors: always start at v = 0 and are shorter than the ground floor, so they stay at ground level whatever the height.
        • 

      • Roof: deck quad at y = h; parapet = 4 thin boxes; extras are fixed unit boxes at fixed corner offsets (never over the pad); optional helipad = 24-gon disc Ø18 at h + 0.05, yellow ring and white H quads at h + 0.08.
        • 

      • Facade orientation: a facade is built once in local (u, v) coordinates, then placed with yaw about +Y: +Z 0°, +X 90°, −Z 180°, −X 270° (glRotatef). Elevations above are drawn as seen from outside each face.
